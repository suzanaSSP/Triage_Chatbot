# 🏥 Triage Chatbot

### **Please input your symptoms** or clinical vignette below to begin triage assessment.

---

Welcome to the **5-Level Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Decision-Support System**.

- 🔴 **Level 1 - Resuscitation**: Immediate life-saving intervention required (0 min wait)
- 🟠 **Level 2 - Emergent**: High risk, severe pain, or acute distress (< 10 min wait)
- 🟡 **Level 3 - Urgent**: Requires $\ge 2$ hospital resources (< 30 min wait)
- 🟢 **Level 4 - Less Urgent**: Requires $1$ hospital resource (< 60 min wait)
- 🔵 **Level 5 - Non-Urgent**: Requires $0$ hospital resources (< 120 min wait)
